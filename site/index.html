How matching works. We search each candidate's voters' guide statement and the issues and about pages of their campaign website for the topics you picked, and show the sentences that mention them, word for word, with a link to the source. It finds what candidates chose to talk about, not where they stand. If a topic isn't mentioned, that doesn't mean they have no position, so follow the links to learn more.

Where this comes from. Candidate statements, photos, contact info and ballot measure text come word for word from the WA Secretary of State online voters' guide, which candidates and committees write themselves. Website quotes come from the campaign site each candidate listed in that guide. Campaign money for state and local candidates comes from WA Public Disclosure Commission open data; candidates for Congress report to the FEC. Districts come from King County GIS. VoterHelp is an independent, nonpartisan project, not affiliated with any government office or campaign. Your official ballot is the final word on which races you vote in.
